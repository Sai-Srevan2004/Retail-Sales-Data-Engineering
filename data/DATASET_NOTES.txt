Retail sales CSV dataset
=========================

Source row counts:
customers_initial.csv: 1,000
products_initial.csv: 100
stores_initial.csv: 30
sales_initial.csv: 10,000
customers_batch_01.csv: 25
products_batch_01.csv: 5
sales_batch_01.csv: 200
customers_batch_02.csv: 24
products_batch_02.csv: 5
sales_batch_02.csv: 200

Column names and order match the project table definitions.

Intentional quality scenarios include whitespace, inconsistent casing, blank
values, invalid emails, invalid dates/timestamps, non-numeric and non-positive
prices, invalid boolean-like values, non-positive quantities, negative or
excessive discounts, and duplicate business keys / sale IDs.

Batch scenarios:
- Batch 01: C00001 email change; C00002 unchanged; C01022 invalid email;
  duplicate C01023; P00001/P00002 price changes; P00101/P00103 new;
  P00102 invalid price; a sale for C99999 before the customer arrives.
- Batch 02: real C99999 customer arrives; C00003 region/segment change;
  C01044 invalid email; P00003 price change; P00104-P00107 new products;
  one excessive-discount sale and one duplicate sale ID.

All data is synthetic and deterministic. No real customer information is used.
